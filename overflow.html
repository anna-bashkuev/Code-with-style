Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious will not be broken unless a normal space appears. Useful for CJK scripts that want to avoid intra‑word hyphenation.

white‑space: normal

The default setting collapses multiple spaces, removes leading & trailing whitespace, and ignores line‑break characters in the source. The Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious word still behaves like in the previous examples because the wrapping decision is governed by overflow-wrap.

Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious will stay on one line – the browser simply refuses to break the line even when it overflows the viewport. A horizontal scrollbar appears if the line is too long.

In this mode every space, including trailing ones, is treated as a potential line‑break point. The Dezember‑long‑word is still wrapped, but you can now see that the very last space in the line becomes a valid break.
